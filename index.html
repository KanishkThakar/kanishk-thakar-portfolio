Guiding
Principles

Six ideas that guide how I learn, build and collaborate. A personal compass for working at the intersection of people and technology.

6

BEGIN WITH PEOPLE

Start with the person behind the problem. Technology earns its place when it makes an experience more useful, understandable or humane.

LEARN THROUGH BUILDING

Give an idea a working form. Prototypes expose assumptions and turn abstract questions into something that can be explored.

CONNECT DISCIPLINES

Look across engineering, finance and human wellbeing. A different perspective can reveal what a single discipline leaves unseen.

MAKE COMPLEXITY CLEAR

Move from a complicated system to an understandable experience. Clarity matters in an interface, a conversation and a business decision.

BUILD WITH INTENTION

Connect ambition with the practical work of execution. Finance, operations and partnerships help a product find its way into the world.

STAY A STUDENT

Keep asking better questions. Learning is a continuing part of the work, across technology, strategy and the people around me.
